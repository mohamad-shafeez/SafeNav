    Under the Hood
    Science-backed
    Risk Intelligence
    SafeNav's risk engine is not a simple weather lookup. It applies the NOAA Heat Index formula to calculate how hot it actually feels, then models Cumulative Exposure Strain — how long you're exposed, how fast you're moving, and how your medical history amplifies danger.

    All this runs on our Python/Flask backend in under 5 seconds, pulling from OpenWeatherMap and WAQI simultaneously.

    Personalization

    Built Around Your Health

    Not everyone faces the same risk. SafeNav adapts its thresholds and advisories to your specific medical profile.

    🧑‍💼
    Standard
    Baseline thresholds for healthy adults. Get full situational awareness without medical-specific adjustments.

    🫁
    Respiratory / Asthma
    Tightened AQI thresholds and faster strain accumulation. AI advisories include inhaler reminders and route alternatives.

    ❤️‍🔥
    Cardiovascular
    Heat and humidity carry extra weight for cardiovascular users. Advisories recommend rest stops and hydration intervals.
